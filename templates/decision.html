Cost formula

Expected total cost (order, mode) =
      freight[mode]                              assumption
    + P(late | order, mode) x penalty(order)     model x assumption
    + holding_rate x order_value x transit[mode] assumption
    + speed_value x transit[mode]                assumption

where penalty(order) = fixed + rate x order_value

P(late) comes from the trained model, scored counterfactually with each mode substituted in. Mean transit per mode is measured from the data. Every monetary coefficient is an assumption you control.

Why there is a time term — and what happens without it

The brief frames expected cost as shipping cost + P(late) × cost of late delivery. Applied literally to this dataset that formula is degenerate: Standard Class has both the lowest assumed freight cost and the lowest observed late rate, so it dominates every alternative for every order under every assumption.
