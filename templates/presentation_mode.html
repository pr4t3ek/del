Step 11

Live demonstration

Simulator
Open the simulator, change the shipping mode and one order characteristic, and watch the predicted risk, expected cost and recommended mode update from the trained model.
Promise redesign
Tick "override the promise" to model a change to the commitment rather than the operation — the lever from step 8.
Scenario switch
On the decision page, raise the value of delivery speed and watch the optimal policy flip from Standard Class toward the faster modes. This is the uncertainty in the business question made visible.
