Do not read the lowest late rate as the best mode

Late rate vs speed

Standard Class has the lowest late rate and the slowest delivery. Same Day has a higher late rate and is eight times faster. Neither dominates: the "best" mode depends on what a day of delay is worth, which is a business assumption rather than something the data settles.

Average profit by mode

Profit is almost flat across modes. Together with the near-zero correlation between lateness and profit ({% if pl.available %}{{ pl.correlation }}{% endif %}), this means the economic case for any mode cannot be read off historical profit — it has to come from an explicit cost model.

The dataset has no freight-cost field. It carries no carrier, warehouse, or logistics-cost column of any kind, so true total logistics cost cannot be calculated from it. The decision engine uses a transparent scenario cost model whose parameters are stated assumptions and can be changed in the interface. No cost figure in this application is presented as an observed value.
