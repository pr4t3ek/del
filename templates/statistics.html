Numeric factors use Mann-Whitney U rather than a t-test: order value, profit and discount are heavily skewed, so the normality assumption does not hold.

Effect size, not just p-value

Effect size by factor

Cramer's V for categorical factors, rank-biserial r for numeric

Why the ranking is by effect size

At {{ '{:,}'.format(s.n_orders) }} observations, a hypothesis test detects differences far smaller than anything a planner could act on. A p-value answers "is this difference distinguishable from zero?", not "is it big enough to matter?"

The table below reports both. Read the effect-size column first: a factor with p < 0.001 and Cramer's V of 0.02 is a statistically real but operationally irrelevant difference.
